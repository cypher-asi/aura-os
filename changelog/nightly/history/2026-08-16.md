## 7:16 PM — Kimi K3 available with Moonshot pricing wired in

Moonshot's Kimi K3 is now a selectable model in Aura, with provider inference, key normalization, and per-token pricing plumbed end to end.

- Added Kimi K3 to the model catalog so it appears in the chat model picker alongside existing options. (`c86d93d`)
- Introduced Moonshot as a recognized provider, with key normalization for both `aura-kimi-k3` and `moonshot/kimi-k3` identifiers so routing and pricing lookups resolve consistently. (`c86d93d`)
- Wired in published Kimi K3 rates ($3/M input, $15/M output, $3/M cache write, $0.30/M cache read) and confirmed via tests that cached prompt tokens are billed at the discounted rate rather than double-charged. (`c86d93d`)
