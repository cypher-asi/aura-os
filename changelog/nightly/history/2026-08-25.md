# Chat transcript stays put while you type ahead

- Date: `2026-08-25`
- Channel: `nightly`
- Version: `0.1.0-nightly.806.1`
- Release: https://github.com/cypher-asi/aura-os/releases/tag/v0.1.0-nightly.806.1

Today's nightly focuses on two long-standing chat annoyances: the transcript scrolling out from under you mid-response, and follow-up prompts disappearing while an answer was still streaming. Both are fixed, and queued prompts now appear inline with a clear "Queued" state.

## 12:56 PM — Queued prompts stay visible and scroll behaves during streaming

Two chat fixes land together: auto-follow no longer re-arms itself mid-response, and prompts submitted while a reply is still streaming now render in the transcript with a Queued indicator instead of vanishing until their turn.

- Auto-follow now stays off for the rest of a posting session once you scroll up to read — reaching the bottom again (via a late token or layout flush) no longer silently re-engages follow and yanks you back down. A fresh send or explicit jump-to-bottom starts a new follow session. (`00ae87e`)
- Prompts submitted while a response is still streaming are now appended to the transcript immediately as queued display messages, rather than only appearing once the previous turn finishes. (`2ab8c6c`)
- User message bubbles show a subtle "Queued" delivery status (with an ARIA status role) while they wait behind the active response, so it's clear the send was accepted. (`2ab8c6c`)
- Chat send now threads a stable clientMessageId through ChatPanel, ChatSurface, and the agent/chat stream hooks so queued prompts keep a consistent identity from submit through delivery. (`2ab8c6c`)

## Highlights

- Auto-scroll no longer fights you during a streaming response
- Queued follow-up prompts stay visible in the transcript with a delivery status
