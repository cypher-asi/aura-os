# Welcome and daily credits now reach updated users

- Date: `2026-05-17`
- Channel: `nightly`
- Version: `0.1.0-nightly.537.1`
- Release: https://github.com/cypher-asi/aura-os/releases/tag/v0.1.0-nightly.537.1

A small but meaningful auth fix closes a gap where users who updated Aura without logging out never received their welcome and daily credits. The grant now also runs on session restore, so credits land the next time the app opens.

## 11:28 AM — Signup credit grant runs on session restore

Welcome and daily credits are now issued when a session is restored, not only on fresh login or registration, so users who update in place are no longer skipped.

- Users who update Aura without logging out now receive their welcome and daily credits on the next app open, since grant_signup_credits also fires from get_session in addition to the login and register handlers. (`2ddab2c`)
- The grant runs as a fire-and-forget background task and remains idempotent via z-billing's signup_grant_at check, so repeated session restores won't double-grant or slow down the auth response. (`2ddab2c`)

## Highlights

- Signup credits now granted on session restore
- Idempotent, fire-and-forget — no impact on response time
