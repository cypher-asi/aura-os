# New chat resets cleanly and transcripts stop double-showing prompts

- Date: `2026-07-24`
- Channel: `nightly`
- Version: `0.1.0-nightly.775.1`
- Release: https://github.com/cypher-asi/aura-os/releases/tag/v0.1.0-nightly.775.1

A focused nightly aimed at two long-standing chat annoyances: starting a new chat now reliably clears Council settings on the destination lane, and the transcript projector no longer leaves duplicate optimistic prompts hanging around once the assistant reply has been persisted.

## 3:45 PM — Fresh-chat Council state and transcript reconciliation fixes

Starting a new chat now eagerly resets Council configuration on the deterministic fresh lane, and the conversation projector correctly retires optimistic user messages once their assistant reply is persisted.

- New Chat now clears Council count, models, mechanism, and answer strategy on the destination `agent:<id>:fresh` lane in addition to the current session, so previously configured multi-model or contrast setups no longer leak into the next conversation — this also repairs stale preferences left behind by builds predating the persisted-partition migration. (`f680f74`)
- The conversation projector now drops a turn's optimistic user bubble once a matching assistant message has been persisted to history, eliminating the duplicated-prompt flicker at the end of a streamed reply while still preserving genuinely repeated prompts that haven't been confirmed yet. (`f680f74`)
