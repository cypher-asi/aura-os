# Live video previews land in the Aura Video sidekick

- Date: `2026-05-13`
- Channel: `nightly`
- Version: `0.1.0-nightly.507.1`
- Release: https://github.com/cypher-asi/aura-os/releases/tag/v0.1.0-nightly.507.1

A small but visible polish day for Aura Video: generated clips in the sidekick panel now show a real frame from the video itself instead of a generic placeholder, making it much easier to tell them apart at a glance.

## 2:28 AM — Frame-accurate thumbnails for generated videos

The Aura Video sidekick panel now renders a still frame from each generated clip as its thumbnail, with a safe fallback when playback isn't available.

- Generated videos in the Aura Video sidekick panel now display an actual frame from the clip — pulled via a media fragment at the 0.5s mark with preload=auto — instead of a generic Film icon, making clips far easier to identify visually. (`f3e468a`)
- Added a graceful fallback: if the video URL is missing or the preview fails to load, the panel reverts to the Film icon so the layout stays intact. (`f3e468a`)

## Highlights

- Generated videos now show real frame thumbnails in the sidekick
- Graceful Film-icon fallback when a video URL is missing or fails to load
