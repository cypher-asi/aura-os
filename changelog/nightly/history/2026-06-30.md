# Leaderboard stays live while it's open

- Date: `2026-06-30`
- Channel: `nightly`
- Version: `0.1.0-nightly.716.1`
- Release: https://github.com/cypher-asi/aura-os/releases/tag/v0.1.0-nightly.716.1

A focused nightly that fixes a long-standing staleness bug in the feed's leaderboard: reopening the view now pulls fresh data, and a periodic refresh keeps the standings current while you're looking at them.

## 2:42 AM — Feed leaderboard refreshes on open and polls while visible

The feed's leaderboard no longer shows cached entries until the app is restarted — it now refetches every time you open it and stays live via a periodic refresh that's cleanly torn down on close.

- Revisiting the leaderboard now fetches fresh entries on mount instead of relying on a once-per-app-lifetime init, so standings reflect current data without restarting Aura. (`4b9dcfd`)
- While the leaderboard is on screen, a 60-second refresh keeps it live; the interval is stopped on unmount so nothing keeps polling in the background. (`4b9dcfd`, `552e072`)
- New tests lock in the open/close behavior and the start–tick–stop lifecycle of the poll, guarding against regressions where the leaderboard could go stale or leak a background interval. (`2063240`, `552e072`)

## Highlights

- Leaderboard refreshes on every open, not just first launch
- Live 60s polling while the view is mounted, torn down on close
