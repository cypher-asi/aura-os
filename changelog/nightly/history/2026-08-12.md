## 7:22 AM — Claude Sonnet 5 pricing now follows its introductory schedule

Sonnet 5 pricing is now date-aware, applying introductory rates until September 1, 2026 UTC and standard rates after, with live cost estimates wired through the shared resolver.

- The Live Run banner in the Process sidekick no longer hardcodes Sonnet pricing and instead calls resolvePricing, so on-screen cost estimates match the currently effective schedule. (`6259444`)
